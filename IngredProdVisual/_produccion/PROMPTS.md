# Prompts de las fotografías de ingredientes

## Modo

Imágenes nuevas generadas con la herramienta integrada `imagegen` (sin CLI).

## Estructura común

Cada solicitud específica se combinó con este prompt común:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: ingredient hero photography for a premium Instagram pet-food product feature. Scene/backdrop: warm light beige limestone surface, completely clean. Style: highly photorealistic upscale grocery editorial flat lay, tasteful and non-graphic. Composition: wide horizontal 16:9, direct overhead camera, exactly three ingredient groups, balanced triangular arrangement, every group fully visible and immediately recognizable, generous safe margins. Lighting: soft natural studio light with consistent subtle shadows. Text: none. Avoid: packaging, kibble, pets, hands, labels, logos, typography, garnish, cutlery, napkins, extra foods or hidden ingredients.

## Solicitudes específicas

1. Show exactly one raw boneless chicken breast, one lean raw red-meat beef cut representing meat source, and one small ceramic bowl of uncooked white rice.
2. Show exactly one raw boneless chicken breast, one clean raw white-fish fillet, and one lean raw beef cut.
3. Show exactly one lean raw red-meat cut, one raw boneless chicken breast, and one small ceramic bowl of whole wheat grains.
4. Show exactly one small ceramic bowl of whole wheat grains, one raw boneless chicken breast, and one small ceramic bowl containing yellow corn kernels with a visible portion of fine yellow cornmeal.
5. Show exactly one small matte ceramic bowl containing clean raw chicken organ cuts such as liver, heart and gizzard to represent chicken by-products, one lean raw red-meat cut representing meat source, and one small ceramic bowl of whole wheat grains.
6. Show exactly one lean raw beef cut, one raw boneless chicken breast, and one small clear glass bowl of golden rendered poultry oil. Keep the oil clearly separated and recognizable as the third element.
7. Show exactly one small matte ceramic bowl containing clean raw chicken organ cuts such as liver, heart and gizzard to represent chicken by-products, one small ceramic bowl of whole wheat grains, and one small ceramic bowl containing yellow corn kernels with a visible portion of fine yellow cornmeal.
8. Show exactly one raw boneless chicken breast, one small ceramic bowl of whole wheat grains, and one small ceramic bowl of uncooked white rice.
9. Show exactly one lean raw red-meat cut representing meat source, one clean raw white-fish fillet, and one small ceramic bowl of uncooked white rice.
10. Show exactly one tasteful grouping of a lean raw red-meat cut beside a clean beef marrow bone to represent meat-and-bone source as a single element, one raw boneless chicken breast, and one small ceramic bowl of whole wheat grains. Keep the meat and bone touching as one ingredient grouping.
11. Show exactly one lean raw beef cut, one raw boneless chicken breast, and one small ceramic bowl of bright green peas.
12. Show exactly one fresh Atlantic salmon fillet, one clean raw white-fish fillet, and one raw boneless chicken breast.
