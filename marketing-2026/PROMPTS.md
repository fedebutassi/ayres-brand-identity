# Fondos generados para la campaña

Modo utilizado: herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes, sin CLI ni modelo alternativo. Los textos, logos y envases se añadieron posteriormente con el render determinístico de la campaña.

## Familia · perro y gato

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical 4:5 Instagram campaign background for AYRES Pet Supply
Primary request: Create an original, photorealistic emotional lifestyle photo of a joyful medium-sized dog and an elegant adult cat together inside a bright contemporary Argentine home, both looking warmly toward their human just outside the frame, conveying that pets are family.
Scene/backdrop: modern but lived-in Córdoba home, warm stone and cream interior, subtle natural textures, no visible luxury cues.
Style/medium: premium candid editorial pet photography, natural and believable, not staged advertising stock.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5, pets grouped in the lower-right half, generous clean negative space across the upper-left and left side for Spanish campaign copy; both animals fully visible enough to read their expressions.
Lighting/mood: soft late-afternoon window light, warm emotional connection, optimistic and trustworthy.
Color palette: warm cream, stone, soft charcoal and restrained natural green accents compatible with #3DB870.
Constraints: realistic anatomy, authentic fur, original content, no food bowls, no product bags, no human faces, no text, no logos, no watermark.
Avoid: artificial studio backdrop, overly saturated colors, costumes, collars with readable tags, distorted paws, duplicate animals, typography.
```

## Asesoramiento en pet shop

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical 4:5 Instagram campaign background for AYRES Pet Supply B2B lead generation
Primary request: Create an original photorealistic editorial image of an independent Argentine pet shop owner in their early forties, warmly advising a customer inside a modern neighborhood pet shop in Córdoba. The scene should convey trust, expertise, commercial partnership and approachable service.
Scene/backdrop: clean local pet shop with softly blurred shelves and generic unbranded pet-care products; no recognizable brand packaging.
Style/medium: premium documentary-style business photography, natural expressions and believable environment, not corporate stock photography.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5; people and shop context concentrated on the right half and lower-right; generous darker, visually quiet negative space on the left and upper-left for Spanish headline and CTA.
Lighting/mood: soft daylight with subtle warm practical lights, professional, optimistic, authentic.
Color palette: warm charcoal, cream, wood and restrained natural green accents compatible with #3DB870.
Constraints: realistic hands and faces, adult subjects, no readable labels, no text, no logos, no watermark, no visible price signs.
Avoid: supermarket scale, luxury boutique, veterinarian scrubs, cash exchange, staged handshake, exaggerated smiles, distorted hands, typography.
```

## Pregunta de comunidad

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical 4:5 Instagram social engagement campaign background for AYRES Pet Supply
Primary request: Create an original photorealistic, irresistibly engaging portrait of one expressive happy dog and one curious cat leaning toward the camera as if reacting to the viewer, designed to stop the scroll and invite comments.
Scene/backdrop: softly blurred bright outdoor patio of an Argentine home, natural plants and warm stone, no identifiable location.
Style/medium: premium candid pet photography with genuine personality, crisp eyes and detailed fur, playful but sophisticated.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5; animal faces and upper bodies in the lower two-thirds, balanced side by side; generous clean negative space across the top third for a large Spanish question; safe margins on all sides.
Lighting/mood: bright soft daylight, joyful, friendly, warm, energetic.
Color palette: cream, warm charcoal, natural fur tones and restrained green accents compatible with #3DB870.
Constraints: realistic anatomy and whiskers, exactly one dog and one cat, no collars with readable tags, no props, no food, no text, no logos, no watermark.
Avoid: exaggerated cartoon expressions, fisheye distortion, costumes, tongues unnaturally large, duplicated limbs, typography.
```
