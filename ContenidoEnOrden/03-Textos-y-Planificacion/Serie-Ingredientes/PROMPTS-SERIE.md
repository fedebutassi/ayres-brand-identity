# Prompts de generación — serie ingredientes

## Modo utilizado

Generación de imágenes nuevas con la herramienta integrada `imagegen` (sin CLI).

## 1. Salmón, pollo y huevo / cordero, arroz y arveja

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: ingredient photography source for premium Instagram pet-food creatives. Primary request: Create a premium commercial food photograph with two compact ingredient groups for a side-by-side product comparison. Scene/backdrop: warm light beige limestone surface, completely clean. Subject: LEFT group contains exactly one fresh Atlantic salmon fillet, one raw boneless chicken breast, and two clean brown eggs in a small off-white ceramic bowl; RIGHT group contains exactly one lean raw lamb cut, one small ceramic bowl of uncooked brown rice, and one small ceramic bowl of bright green peas. Style/medium: highly photorealistic upscale grocery editorial flat lay. Composition/framing: wide horizontal 16:9, camera directly overhead, clear empty vertical gap at center, every ingredient fully visible, left ingredients contained entirely in left half and right ingredients contained entirely in right half. Lighting/mood: soft natural studio light, consistent subtle shadows, fresh and premium. Color palette: warm beige, salmon coral, muted cream, natural green. Text: none. Constraints: exactly the specified ingredients, visually recognizable, balanced scale, no objects crossing the center. Avoid: packaging, pet food kibble, pets, hands, labels, logos, typography, garnish, cutlery, napkins, hidden or overlapping ingredients.

## 3. Pollo, arroz y fuente de gluten de maíz

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: ingredient hero photography for an individual premium Instagram pet-food feature. Primary request: Create a refined commercial ingredient photograph centered on the three leading ingredient sources of a chicken-and-rice adult dog formula. Scene/backdrop: warm light beige limestone surface with generous clean negative space. Subject: exactly one raw boneless chicken breast, one small matte ceramic bowl filled with uncooked white rice, and one small matte ceramic bowl containing yellow corn kernels together with a clearly visible portion of fine yellow cornmeal representing the source of corn gluten. Style/medium: highly photorealistic premium grocery editorial flat lay. Composition/framing: wide horizontal 16:9, direct overhead camera, the three ingredient elements grouped elegantly but not overlapping, each fully visible and immediately recognizable, composition weighted slightly left with clean space on right. Lighting/mood: soft natural studio light, consistent subtle shadows, clean and confident. Color palette: warm cream, soft pink, white and muted gold. Text: none. Constraints: exactly the specified food elements. Avoid: packaging, kibble, pets, hands, labels, logos, typography, garnish, cutlery, napkins, vegetables, hidden ingredients.

## Criterio de uso

Las fotografías muestran la fuente visual del ingrediente. Las piezas finales conservan en texto la denominación exacta declarada por cada producto, por ejemplo “harina de salmón” o “gluten de maíz”.
