# Prompt de generación — ingredientes explícitos

## Modo

Generación de imagen nueva con la herramienta integrada `imagegen` (sin CLI).

## Prompt final

Create a premium commercial food photography image for a pet food ingredient comparison, wide horizontal 16:9 composition. Warm light beige stone background, soft natural studio light, realistic appetizing raw ingredients, clean editorial flat lay, no packaging, no hands, no animals, no text, no labels, no logos, no decorative props. Arrange EXACTLY two compact ingredient clusters side by side with a clear empty gap between them. LEFT CLUSTER must visibly include all three items at once: one fresh Atlantic salmon fillet, one raw boneless chicken breast, and one small ceramic bowl filled with uncooked brown rice. RIGHT CLUSTER must visibly include all three items at once: one lean raw lamb cut, one small ceramic bowl filled with uncooked brown rice, and one small ceramic bowl filled with bright green peas. Every ingredient must be fully visible and easy to recognize, with balanced scale and no overlaps hiding any item. Camera directly overhead, consistent shadows, high-end grocery campaign photography, photorealistic, crisp details, muted premium palette. Exactly six ingredient elements total: salmon, chicken, brown rice on left; lamb, brown rice, green peas on right.

## Uso en la pieza

La fotografía generada se dividió visualmente en dos paneles y se combinó con las fotografías reales de los envases y las declaraciones de ingredientes existentes en `productos.json`.
